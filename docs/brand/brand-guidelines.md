# Hugin brand guidelines

## Brand idea

Hugin is a watchful raven in profile: calm, intelligent, and scanning ahead. The silhouette communicates observation and useful discovery without literal radar rings, Viking ornament, or mascot styling.

## Responsive marks

| Context | Mark | Minimum size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing, GitHub, documentation, app header | Primary / 1B Corvid | 32 px |
| Favicons and compact UI | Micro / 1H Hybrid | 16 px |

Do not use the micro mark at large display sizes. Do not force the primary mark below 32 px; its throat details are intentionally removed from the micro form.

## Color

| Token | Value | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ground | `#0A0806` | Warm near-black brand surface |
| Ember | `#D66A30` | Primary dark-theme mark/accent |
| Light-theme ember | `#9E3E16` | Accessible mark/accent on light surfaces |
| Ivory | `#FBF7EF` | Primary text on dark surfaces |
| Muted | `#CFC5B4` | Supporting text |

Use one ember family only. Do not add gradients, shadows, metallic effects, or a second accent hue.

## Typography

- Display and wordmark: **Space Grotesk 700**
- Body and supporting copy: **Figtree 500**

## Clear space

Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least the eye height. Around a full lockup, use at least half the mark's head height.

## Backgrounds

- Dark surfaces: ember mark + ivory wordmark.
- Light surfaces: light-theme ember mark + ground wordmark.
- Monochrome variants are for constrained production only.

## Do not

- Stretch, rotate, outline, crop, or recolor individual parts.
- add runes, helmets, knotwork, radar arcs, or decorative feathers.
- combine the primary head with the micro neck or vice versa.
- use another bird silhouette as a substitute.
